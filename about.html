About Me

I am Dave, the designer behind Xaint.XQL.

I am a creative designer and frontend-focused builder who cares deeply about clarity, visual storytelling, and turning ideas into polished web experiences that people actually enjoy using.

My work sits at the intersection of design taste and implementation. I love shaping how things look, how they flow, and how they feel when someone finally lands on the page.

My Story.

I started with a strong curiosity for how digital products look and communicate. What first pulled me in was not just code, but the way good design can make something feel obvious, engaging, and memorable.

Over time, that curiosity became a working process. I learned to think about hierarchy, spacing, typography, color, and interaction as a complete system, not isolated details. That is what pushed me toward becoming the kind of designer who also understands how the interface gets built.

What drives me

I enjoy creating experiences that feel modern, expressive, and easy to navigate. I care about strong first impressions, but I also care about whether the design still feels good after someone uses it for a while.

How I think

I approach design with both emotion and structure. I want an interface to feel beautiful, but I also want it to solve a problem, guide attention, and support a clear user goal.

What Defines Me As A Designer.

Intentional Visual Taste

I care about details that make a design feel sharp, balanced, and memorable rather than generic.

Clear Structure

I like building layouts with strong hierarchy so users know where to look, what matters, and what to do next.

Frontend Awareness

I design with implementation in mind, which helps me create ideas that are not only attractive but practical to build.

User-Centered Thinking

I want the final experience to feel intuitive, useful, and smooth for the person on the other side of the screen.
