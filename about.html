A little
about me

I'm Dave, a Full Stack Developer based in Abuja.

Ask me anything

What kind of work do you like doing?

I care about clarity, visual storytelling and turning ideas into polished web experiences people actually enjoy using. I love shaping how something looks, how it flows, and how it feels when someone lands on the page.

How did you get started?

I started with a strong curiosity for how digital products look and communicate. What pulled me in was not just code, but how good design can make something feel obvious, engaging and memorable. That curiosity became a process: hierarchy, spacing, typography, color and interaction as one system, not isolated details.

Do you build things too, or only design them?

Both. I'm a full stack developer, so I design with implementation in mind and what gets approved is what actually ships. I work across frontend and backend, from the first wireframe to a deployed product.

What do you care about most in the work?

I approach design with both emotion and structure. I want an interface to feel beautiful, but I also want it to solve a problem, guide attention and support a clear goal. A strong first impression matters, and so does whether it still feels good after someone has used it for a while.

What do you do off the clock?

I play a lot of FC and COD. Long walks too, that's where most of my ideas show up.
